## About HCTM

Have Compassion on Them Missions is a Christian ministry dedicated to winning souls and caring for missionaries. Our burden is to reach unreached people and places with the gospel of Jesus Christ, strengthen discipleship among believers, and stand alongside suffering saints and missionaries in need.

We value faithful service, including work carried out quietly and away from public attention. Our focus is on the people being reached and supported, rather than recognition for ourselves.

## Our mandate

### Restore grassroots discipleship

Encourage believers to make disciples in their everyday communities and help others grow in their walk with Christ.

### Revive the missionary spirit

Awaken a renewed burden for souls and a willingness to serve Christ through going, praying, and supporting those on the mission field.

### Reach the unreached

Bring the gospel to unreached people and places, while offering practical support to missionaries and suffering saints.

## Our work

HCTM has a network of missionaries across the states engaged in discipleship, alongside student missionaries undergoing training. We seek more believers who are willing to go, serve, and help others come to know Christ.

Missionaries on the field also need people who will stand with them in prayer and practical support. Our work brings these commitments together: reaching souls, training workers, and caring for those who serve.

## Pray with us

Committed intercession is central to our mission. We pray for the salvation of souls, the strengthening of missionaries, and the advancement of the gospel in unreached communities.

If you are a believer willing to make a consistent commitment to prayer, contact us to learn how to take part.
